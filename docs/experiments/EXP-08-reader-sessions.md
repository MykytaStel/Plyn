# EXP-08 Sessions with real readers

Status: not started · Feeds: [0.28](../ROADMAP.md#0280--accessibility-audit), [0.30](../ROADMAP.md#0300--quality-gate) · Time box: 2 weeks, 5 sessions

## Question

Where do readers with ADHD or dyslexia get stuck in Plyn, and which features do they actually use and keep on?

## Why it matters

Automated tests prove that Plyn works as built. Only readers can show whether it helps. Five sessions usually reveal most of the biggest usability problems.

## Plan

1. Recruit 5 adult readers who self-identify as having ADHD and/or dyslexia, Ukrainian and English speakers mixed. No diagnosis is asked for.
2. Each session (≈ 40 min, remote or in person): a short warm-up, then tasks on their own phone:
   1. Install Plyn and open the sample.
   2. Paste an article of their choice and read for 5 minutes.
   3. Make it more comfortable to read (find any setting).
   4. Park a thought, then come back to the text.
   5. Find where they stopped after closing the app.
3. Observe where they hesitate. Ask afterwards: what helped, what was too much, what they would turn off.
4. Write findings as issues, ranked by how many readers hit them.

## Success criteria

- 5 sessions completed and written up.
- Every finding hit by 2+ readers has a roadmap decision (fix, design, or "intentional").

## Ethics and privacy

- Written consent; readers can stop at any time.
- Do not record the texts they read or screenshots of their library. Notes describe behaviour, not content.
- No diagnosis questions. Thank participants (for example a small gift card), decided before recruiting.
