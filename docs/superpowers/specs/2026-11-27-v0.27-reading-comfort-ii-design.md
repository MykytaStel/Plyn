# 0.27.0 — Reading comfort II

Status: shipped (syllable hints pending EXP-03) · 2026-11-27 · [ROADMAP](../../ROADMAP.md#0270--reading-comfort-ii) · experiment: [EXP-03](../../experiments/EXP-03-ukrainian-syllable-hints.md) (in progress)

## Why

Plyn's readers have ADHD or dyslexia, and they are easily overloaded. 0.27 adds small, optional helps for dyslexia (syllable hints) and time blindness (a time-left estimate that uses your own pace). It also closes the loop on Thought Parking: you can find the thoughts from a session, and you can export them.

At the same time, Settings has grown into one long sheet: presets, five sections and about 20 controls. 0.27 would add 2–3 more. The owner said it plainly: everything there works, "but it's a bit much, and I don't know how to fit it all so that it's comfortable for a person with ADHD". So 0.27 also reorganises Settings (option B, chosen by the owner in the mockup session) and does a design pass over the rest of the app.

Product rule (AGENTS.md): calm and uncluttered beats feature-rich. Every item below either adds no control to a main screen or removes one.

## Goals

1. Settings are calm to open: a short first screen, and every section fits on one phone screen.
2. Syllable hints for long Ukrainian words, if EXP-03 passes. Off by default.
3. Time left is based on the reader's own pace and can be hidden.
4. At the end of a session, a quiet way to see the thoughts parked during it.
5. Thought Parking exports to Markdown.
6. A design pass: fix what looks broken or noisy on a phone (list below).

## Non-goals

- English syllable hints. English syllabification is irregular and needs a heavy dictionary (EXP-03 "What we know"). Ukrainian only.
- New reader toolbar buttons. Nothing new goes in the reader header.
- iOS speech rate steps (moved here from 0.26). They stay "later". The Sound section is being rebuilt anyway, so it is a cheap place to add them in a later release.
- Reader sessions with other people (EXP-08 belongs to 0.28).

## Anti-goals (a failure even if it works)

- A time estimate that pressures: "3 h 40 min left" on a book, a countdown, red text, "you're slower than usual".
- Syllable marks that are wrong. A wrong split teaches the wrong shape of a word, and that is worse than no split.
- A Settings tree so deep that people can't find things. One level of sections, no more.
- Anything that changes the text itself. Copy, search, TTS, offsets and backups must see exactly the same characters.

## Constraints

- All invariants in AGENTS.md hold. Most relevant here:
  - progress stays a char offset;
  - new settings get defaults in `plyn_settings_v1`, with no new key;
  - an optional stats field doesn't bump the backup schema but gets a fixture in `spec/backups/`;
  - `src/core/` stays language-free and DOM-free;
  - every string exists in `uk` and `en`.
- New sheets and views use `role="dialog"`/headings, `useDialogFocus` and `useBackLayer`. Visual snapshots are updated on purpose in the same PR.
- Copy is calm, second person ("ти"), and makes no claims of proven effects.

## Approaches

### Settings layout (the owner picked B)

- **A. Essentials on top, the rest in accordions.** One page. Theme, size, card length and presets are always visible, and the rarer groups collapse. Rejected: an open accordion makes the page long again, and a collapsed group hides what is switched on inside it.
- **B. A list of sections, each on its own screen (chosen).** The first screen shows presets plus five rows. Each row has a one-line summary of its current state, and a tap opens a short screen for that section. Cost: one more tap to reach rare options.
- **C. Move options to where they are used.** Sound would move into the reader's listen button, and backup and language to the library. Rejected: people would no longer know where to look, and it is the biggest change of habits.

### Where the reader's pace lives

- **Stats in IndexedDB (`UserProgressStats.readingPace`, chosen).** It is reading data, not a preference. It is already in backups, and it is written by the same atomic stats update.
- localStorage under a new key. Rejected: it is not in backups, and it would be one more key to version.
- Settings. Rejected: it isn't something the reader sets, and the settings loader would have to protect it from being overwritten.

### Syllable display

This is decided by EXP-03, not up front. The prototype compares three displays: a middle dot (`са·мо·стій·ність`), a thin gap, and an alternating subtle tone. All three are display-only: CSS pseudo-elements or non-text spans, so the characters never change. The owner is the judge, and preview viewers can comment.

### 2. Syllable hints (behind EXP-03)

EXP-03 runs first, inside this release, with its criteria changed honestly because there are no outside readers yet:
- **≥ 98 % accuracy** on `spec/syllable-cases.json` (at least 500 Ukrainian words, written by hand from school materials and dictionaries; covering clusters, `й`, `ь`, the apostrophe, `дж`/`дз`, compounds). This is a hard gate.
- **The owner's judgement** replaces "2 of 3 readers": they try the three displays on long texts on their own phone and pick one or none. Preview viewers may comment. EXP-03 records this as a deviation from the original plan.
- **< 2 ms** extra render time per card.

**Which rule is "correct"** (builder's call, made in the design): the school rule for dividing words into syllables *for reading* (open syllables, as in «се-стра», «ка-ли-на»), not the rule for line-end hyphenation (перенос), which is morphemic («сес-тра»). The exception is a sonorant (л, м, н, р, й, в) before another consonant: it closes the syllable («сон-це», «вій-сько», «мор-ква»). «дж» and «дз» stay together when they are one sound («хо-джу»). The apostrophe and «ь» stay with the syllable before them. Before the 500 cases are frozen, the owner reviews a sample of 50 (including every rule above), so the 98 % gate isn't graded only against the builder's own answers. EXP-03 records the source.

**Order** (builder's call): EXP-03 runs on its own branch (`exp/03-syllable-hints`), with the splitter, the cases and the three displays behind a dev-only flag. 0.27 doesn't wait for it. If the owner decides yes before the release, the feature (setting, copy, snapshots) is merged into `feat/v0.27`. Otherwise it ships in a later release. The VoiceOver check is done by the owner on the PR preview, because the builder has no device.

If the gate fails, syllable hints don't ship in 0.27. The rest of the release still ships, and EXP-03 records why.

If it passes:
- Pure `src/core/syllables/syllables.ts`: `splitSyllables(word: string): string[]` (Ukrainian spoken syllables, not hyphenation points) and `syllableBreaks(word): number[]` (the char indices inside the word). It is language-free in the AGENTS.md sense: it returns data, never copy.
- Hints appear only on words with **4 or more syllables** (the EXP-03 guess; the experiment may tune it) in cards whose content language is Ukrainian.
- Setting `syllableHints: boolean`, default `false`, under Підказки, with a calm hint line. It makes no claim of a speed-up.
- Rendering: inside the existing word tokens of `CardSlide`, so it works together with anchor mode (bold lead), sentence focus and the spoken-word highlight. The text nodes keep their exact characters, and marks come from CSS or `aria-hidden` elements. A screen reader reads the whole word once (verified with VoiceOver in the PR).
- No keyboard toggle. It is a "set once" option.

### 3. Time left at your own pace

Today the reader header shows `1 / 9 · ~1 хв`. It is computed at a fixed 180 words per minute, and it re-counts the words of every remaining card on each render.

- **Measuring.** When the reader leaves a card, the time spent on it counts as a pace sample (`words / minutes`) only if all of these hold:
  - it was on screen for at least 1.5 s (skimming or going back doesn't count);
  - the time is capped at 3× the time the current pace expects, so a pause or a phone put down doesn't count;
  - the reader wasn't listening (read-aloud sets its own pace);
  - the page was visible (`visibilitychange`).
- **Storing.** `UserProgressStats.readingPace?: { wordsPerMinute: number; samples: number }` is updated as an exponential moving average and clamped to 60–600 wpm. It goes through the existing atomic stats update.
- **Using.** Until there are 20 samples, the estimate uses the calm default (180 wpm), as today. After that it uses the reader's own pace. There is no visible switch between the two and no notice.
- **What it measures to.** In a document with chapters, the time to the end of the current chapter. Otherwise, the time to the end of the text. This keeps a big book from showing hours. Over 60 minutes it shows whole hours ("~2 год"), never hours plus minutes.
- **Words per card** are computed once per split (cumulative word counts), not on every render.
- **Library cards** (decided by the owner): the time on a library card follows the same rules. It uses the reader's own pace once there is one, it is hidden when `showTimeLeft` is off, and it shows whole hours over 60 minutes. It is the time for the rest of the text, since there is no current chapter in the library. A finished document shows «Прочитано» (design pass item 5).
- **Hiding.** Setting `showTimeLeft: boolean`, default `true` (it is shown today), under Читання. When it is off, the header shows only `1 / 9`.
- **Copy.** It stays `~N хв`. The header's accessible name says what it is ("about 4 min to the end of the chapter"). No comparisons, no "faster than usual".
- Pure helpers go in `src/core/reading/` (pace update, minutes for words with a pace, rounding). They are unit-tested for: no pace yet, very slow, very fast, a paused card, and the clamps.

### 4. Thoughts from this session

- The reader store records `sessionStartedAt` when a document opens. "This session's thoughts" are the thoughts with `createdAt ≥ sessionStartedAt`. There is no schema change.
- **The "Текст прочитано" screen** (`DoneSlide`): if any thoughts were parked during the session, a quiet line appears under the summary, for example «Під час читання ти запаркував 2 думки» with a text button «Переглянути», which opens Thought Parking. With none, nothing is shown.
- **What «Переглянути» shows** (builder's call): the Thought Parking sheet, with this session's thoughts on top under a small heading «З цього читання» and the rest below as today. The heading appears only when the sheet is opened from one of these two links.
- **Leaving early** (back to the library with at least one thought parked this session): the library shows its usual short toast, «2 думки з цього читання» · «Переглянути». It doesn't block anything and isn't repeated.
- The copy uses `plural()` and carries no pressure or guilt.

### 5. Export Thought Parking to Markdown

- Pure `src/core/export/thoughtsMarkdown.ts`, in the same pattern as `bookmarksMarkdown.ts`: thoughts grouped by `docTitle` (thoughts without one go under a neutral heading), newest group first, each thought with its date (formatted through labels passed in, never copy in core).
- An "Експорт у Markdown" button in the Thought Parking sheet, shown only when there are thoughts. File name: `plyn-thoughts-YYYY-MM-DD.md`.
- Thoughts were in backups already. This is a readable copy, not a backup.

### 6. Design pass (designer's calls, from a phone walkthrough at 375 px)

1. **Voice selects:** the native arrow sticks out of the field on some platforms, and the field is taller, has larger text and a darker background than the controls around it. Fix: `appearance: none`, Plyn's own chevron inside the padding, the same height, font size and background as the segmented controls. Under option B, a voice row shows the current voice as its value.
2. **Card hyphenation:** `hyphens: auto` breaks Ukrainian words very often (4 of 7 lines on a phone card) and at wrong places («коро-тку», «чі-ткими»). For dyslexic readers a broken word costs more than a ragged edge. Fix: no automatic hyphenation in cards. Text stays left-aligned and ragged right, and very long words still wrap (`overflow-wrap: break-word`). Reviewed by eye in all four fonts on the phone snapshots.
3. **Library header:** «Бекап» and «Імпорт» sit next to «Моя бібліотека», and «Імпорт» is easy to confuse with importing a file. They move to Settings → Мова і бібліотека as «Зберегти резервну копію» and «Відновити з копії». The library header keeps the title and the count.
4. **"Легкий старт" offers a finished text** («Почати 5 карток» on a document at 100 %). Fix: suggest only unfinished documents, and hide the block when there are none.
5. **A finished document's library card** shows «Картка 9 з 9 · 100% · ~1 хв». Fix: show «Прочитано» and no time.
6. **An achievement toast covers the Done-screen buttons.** Fix: toasts never cover the active screen's actions (they sit above them or wait until the Done screen has settled).
7. **Truncated placeholders:** «Назва (необов'язково — згенерується автомати…» and «Наприклад: відповісти на лис…». Fix: shorter copy that fits at 320 px.
8. **Keyboard check:** after a sheet closes, focus returns to its opener. Check that Space and the arrow keys still turn cards, and don't re-activate the toolbar button. Add a keyboard E2E step and fix it if it fails.
