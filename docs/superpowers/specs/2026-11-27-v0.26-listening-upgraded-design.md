# 0.26.0 — Listening, upgraded

Status: shipped · 2026-11-27 · [ROADMAP](../../ROADMAP.md#0260--listening-upgraded) · experiment: [EXP-02](../../experiments/EXP-02-tts-word-highlighting.md) (decided: yes)

## Why

Listening helps reading only when the eyes can follow the voice. Today Plyn reads a card aloud with no sign of where it is in the text. It always uses the browser's default voice, which on some devices is a poor or English-only one. And it stops for a moment between cards while the feed scrolls. EXP-02 showed that iOS (Safari and Chrome, both WebKit) and Chromium on macOS report word positions accurately for Ukrainian, so a highlight that follows the voice can work.

## What ships

1. **The spoken word is highlighted** (Settings → Sound, off by default).
   - `services/speech/tts.ts` passes `word` boundary events up as `{ start, end }` ranges in card text, and nothing more.
   - The pure helper `core/speech/wordRange.ts` turns a raw boundary into a range: it ignores events whose `charIndex` doesn't start a letter or digit (EXP-02 saw one on «—») and trims trailing punctuation from `charLength` («тихий,» → «тихий»). The helper is language-free and has a test.
   - The card renders the range as a static `<mark class="spoken-word">` style: background only, no movement, and the same look under `prefers-reduced-motion`. It works together with anchor mode and sentence focus. The highlight lives on the active card only, and it doesn't change word spacing or reflow the text.
   - Detection by behaviour, never by user agent: if no word event arrives within 3 s of `start`, the highlight hides for that utterance, and that voice is marked "no word positions" for the session, so later cards don't flash and vanish. EXP-02 saw the first event take 1.2 s on a cold voice, so 1 s would be too short.
   - Where `speechSynthesis` is missing, the switch is disabled with a short hint. Where support is unknown (it can only be known after speaking), the switch stays enabled and the hint says it works with most system voices.
2. **Choose a voice per content language** (Settings → Sound).
   - Two selects, "Voice: Ukrainian" and "Voice: English", each listing only `speechSynthesis.getVoices()` for that language. Network voices are marked, because Plyn must work offline. The first option is "Browser default".
   - The voice list arrives late on some browsers (`voiceschanged`), so the selects update when it does. A saved voice that has disappeared falls back to the default without an error.
   - Choosing a voice speaks one short sentence in it, so the reader can hear it straight away.
   - `speakCardText` takes the voice from the card's *content* language (`detectContentLanguage`), as `lang` does today. `speakSingleWord` (double tap) uses the same voice.
3. **Listening continues across cards without gaps, and stops at a chapter's end.**
   - With auto-advance on, the next card's utterance is queued in `speechSynthesis` as soon as the current one starts, so the voice doesn't wait for the scroll. The feed scrolls to card N+1 when its utterance starts. Session ids keep stale events from the cancelled queue away.
   - **Chapter end:** if the next card starts a new chapter (a `chapters[].offset` falls inside it, decided by the pure `core/reading/chapterBoundary.ts`), listening doesn't queue it. The feed moves to the first card of the new chapter and listening stops there. The chapter's title shows in the reader's usual short notice (`aria-live="polite"`), and ▶ or S continues. Documents without chapters keep reading to the end.
   - Turning a card by hand, leaving the reader or opening a sheet stops listening as today, and clears the queue.

No new control on a main screen (roadmap rule 1): all new options live in Settings. The chapter line is a status, not a control.

## Settings and storage

- `UserSettings` gets `ttsWordHighlight: boolean` (default `false`) and `ttsVoices: { uk?: string; en?: string }` (`voiceURI`, default `{}`) in `plyn_settings_v1`. New fields have defaults, so the key stays the same (invariant 4). The loader drops unknown languages and non-string values.
- Backups carry settings, and `ttsVoices` is an optional field, so `schemaVersion` stays 2. A fixture with the new fields is added to `spec/backups/`. A voice id from another device simply falls back to the default.
- Progress stays a character offset. The highlight and the queue never persist anything.

## Approaches considered

- **Highlight by timing estimate** (words per second from the rate) where boundaries are missing: rejected. EXP-02 says a lagging highlight is worse than none.
- **One utterance per chapter** instead of per card: no gaps at all, but a 30 k-character utterance is cut off on iOS and makes cancel and rate changes coarse. Queueing per card keeps the current model.
- **Pause and continue at a chapter's end**: rejected by the owner in favour of a clear stop.

## Out of scope

- Retuning rate steps for iOS, where the rate isn't linear (EXP-02: 26 s at 0.75, 24 s at 1, 10 s at 1.5 for the same card). Noted for a later release.
- Network or downloadable voices, voice pitch, a per-document voice.
- Android Chrome, Firefox and Windows were not measured. They are covered by the runtime check, not by a promise.

## Tests

- **Unit (node):** `core/speech/wordRange.test.ts` (punctuation events, trailing punctuation, apostrophes «м'яке», out-of-range indexes); `core/reading/chapterBoundary.test.ts` (chapter at card start, in the middle of a card, no chapters, last card).
- **TTS contract (node):** `services/speech/tts.test.ts` against a fake `speechSynthesis` that emits `start` / `boundary` / `end` / `error` on demand: word ranges reach the caller, stale sessions are ignored after `cancel`, the queue speaks card N+1 without a gap, the 3 s rule hides the highlight and marks the voice, and the voice is chosen by content language with a fallback.
- **Settings:** the loader merges the new fields over defaults and rejects bad values; a backup fixture with `ttsVoices` imports.
- **Component (dom):** Settings shows the two voice selects filtered by language and the highlight switch; the card renders `mark.spoken-word` on the right word.
- **E2E:** a stubbed `speechSynthesis` (an init script in `e2e/support/`) on Chromium and WebKit: highlight follows stub boundaries; auto-advance crosses cards and stops at the first card of the next chapter (the `cuj-12` book); progress in IndexedDB matches. The Settings sheet axe scan covers the new controls. Visual snapshot for a card with a highlighted word, updated on purpose.

## Done when

- On iOS Safari and macOS Chromium with a Ukrainian voice, the highlight is never more than one word behind (checked by ear on the PR preview at 0.75, 1, 1.25 and 1.5), and it hides itself where a voice sends no word events.
- Auto-advance plays card to card with no audible gap and stops at a chapter's first card.
- `lint`, `typecheck`, `test`, `build`, `test:e2e` and `budgets` are green.
