# 0.24.0 — PDFs and web articles import well

Status: approved · 2026-11-27 · [ROADMAP](../ROADMAP.md#0240--pdfs-and-web-articles-import-well)

## Why

PDFs imported in 0.15–0.23 join every page into one giant paragraph with page numbers, running headers, and broken hyphenated words (`сло- во`). Web articles from older Ukrainian sites in Windows-1251 arrive mojibake, news pages pull in "read also" blocks, and `/api/extract` still lacks redirect SSRF checks, timeouts, byte caps, and clear signals for login-walled or JS-only pages (`IMP-01`).

## What ships

1. **Pure PDF layout reconstruction (`src/core/parsers/pdfLayout.ts`):**
   - Groups positioned pdf.js text items into visual lines per page.
   - Removes repeated running headers/footers (appearing across ≥ 50 % of pages with digits normalized) and standalone top/bottom page numbers.
   - Rebuilds paragraphs using vertical gaps, first-line indents, heading font sizes, and cross-page sentence continuation.
   - Joins line-end hyphenations (`сло-` / `во` → `слово`) while preserving real Ukrainian and English compound words (`науково-технічний`, `well-known`).
   - Stays pure in `src/core/` with shared fixtures in `spec/parsers/pdf-cases.json`; `pdf.js` remains lazy-loaded.
2. **Hardened and smarter `/api/extract` (`worker/index.ts`, `IMP-01`):**
   - Manual redirect following (`redirect: 'manual'`, max 5 hops) with full SSRF host checking on every hop (IPv4 private/reserved, IPv6 loopback/ULA/link-local/IPv4-mapped, numeric/octal/hex IPv4, trailing-dot hostnames).
   - `AbortSignal.timeout(10_000)` and streamed 2 MB response cap.
   - Charset detection from `Content-Type` and `<meta charset>` (decoding Windows-1251, KOI8-U, ISO-8859-1, UTF-16, UTF-8).
   - Cleaner article extraction: strips menus, cookie banners, sidebars, and "read also" / social blocks.
   - Calm, localized error codes for `login_required`, `js_required`, `too_many_redirects`, `timeout`, `response_too_large`, and `rate_limited`, plus `truncated: true` when a very long article is trimmed at 120,000 characters.
   - Optional Workers Rate Limiting binding (`EXTRACT_RATE_LIMITER`) ready to attach in Cloudflare.

## Out of scope

- Two-column scientific PDFs (documented in EXP-01).
- OCR for scanned image-only PDFs (parked under EXP-05).
- No new controls added to main screens (Roadmap rule 1).

## Tests

- L1 pure unit & golden tests for `pdfLayout.ts` (`spec/parsers/pdf-cases.json`) + 300-page performance sanity test.
- Worker tests (`worker/extract.test.ts`): SSRF matrix (IPv4, IPv6, IPv4-mapped, numeric, redirect-to-private), redirect loop cap, timeout, byte cap, Windows-1251 decoding, saved HTML news/blog/login/JS fixtures, truncation flag, rate limiting.
- Client `urlExtractor.test.ts` updated for new error codes and `truncated` flag; full `lint`, `typecheck`, and `test` green.
